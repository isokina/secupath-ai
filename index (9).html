HELP.TXT // 05 ENTRIES
Q.01Is SecuPath really free?

Yes. Version 1.0 of the Android app is free to download, and the browser lab on this page is free to use.

Q.02Do I need to know Linux already?

No. Every disk starts with a briefing and a set of suggested commands, and the mentor explains what each one does. Type help or hint in the terminal at any time.

Q.03Is the browser lab safe to use?

Completely. The terminal on this page is a simulation: every command is interpreted inside the page and never touches your computer, your files, or a real network.

Q.04Where do my mentor questions go?

When you're online, your question and current lesson title are sent to a free public DeepSeek model. If it can't be reached, the built-in local engine answers instead and nothing leaves the page.

Q.05What will I actually learn?

The everyday fundamentals of defending a Linux host: file permissions and least privilege, log forensics, port and process auditing, HTTP reconnaissance, and firewall containment.
